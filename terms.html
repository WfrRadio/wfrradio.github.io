Personal desktop use

This configuration is intended for its owner's personal Google Drive access. It is not a hosted storage service. Only access accounts and files you are authorized to use. Google Drive and GitHub remain subject to their respective providers' terms and policies.

File operations and availability

File edits, uploads, moves and deletions made through the mount affect Google Drive. Uploads may be buffered locally and finish in the background. Verify completion before disconnecting, and retain independent backups of important files. Network routes, the third-party relay and third-party APIs may become unavailable, and uninterrupted access or permanent OAuth authorization is not promised.
